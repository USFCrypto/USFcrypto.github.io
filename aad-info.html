Lodging

Our workshop hotel is the Sonesta Select Boca Raton, located at 2000 NW Executive Center Circle, Boca Raton, FL 33431. You can reach the hotel at (561) 241-7070. Please visit the hotel website for details about the property and its amenities.

Workshop Venue

Workshop talks will take place in SE 319B, in FAU’s Science Building (SE-43) on the Boca Raton campus.

The building is on the north side of campus, facing the football stadium. Its entrance faces the Recreation Center, near parking lot 4. See FAU’s building directions or view the Science Building (SE-43) on Google Maps for help finding the venue.

Meals and Social Events

Lunch is sponsored on Saturday, October 17, and Sunday, October 18. We have tickets for participants to enjoy lunch at the FAU cafeteria on both days.

The workshop dinner will be at 7pm at TAP 42. Participants will cover the cost of their own dinner.
